🏢Clock In

Your workday builds a tower.

Clock In is a game played with your real workday. Clock in and a new floor goes up. Focus and the windows light. Leave on time and a pay envelope brings a new resident. Overtime earns nothing extra.

How it works

  • Clock in when you start work — by hand, or automatically when you arrive at a workplace you saved.
  • Focus blocks of 15–50 minutes light the four windows of today's floor.
  • Clock out on time to open a pay envelope with a random resident for your tower.
  • Beat the weekly boss with a normal good week. No grinding, no overtime.
  • Day-off tokens keep your streak safe on days you rest (3 a month, 6 with Clock In+).

Help

Restore Clock In+ on a new iPhone. Settings › Clock In+ › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel Clock In+. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Clock In+. Apple handles billing and refunds: request a refund from Apple.

Stop automatic clock-in. In the app: Settings › Workplace › Remove workplace. Or turn off Location for Clock In in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Move or back up your tower. Your tower lives only on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup if you use one; there is no Clock In account or server to restore it from.
