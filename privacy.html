🏢Clock In

Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no server.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Your shifts, your tower and your workplace location stay on your iPhone. We never receive them.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Clock-in and clock-out times, focus blocks and day-off tokens you use.
  • Your tower, residents, trophies and settings (shift hours, workdays, tower style).
  • Optional: the location of one workplace (latitude, longitude and a 150 m radius), only if you choose "save this place".

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Widgets and Live Activities read it on the same device to show your tower and focus timer.

Location

Location is optional. When you save a workplace, the app reads your position once. If you allow "Always", iOS watches that one circle and tells the app when you arrive or leave, so it can clock you in or out. The coordinates and these events never leave your iPhone. Remove the workplace in Settings › Workplace, or switch off Location for the app in iPhone Settings, at any time.

Notifications

Reminders (clock in, clock out on time, focus done) are scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Clock In+ is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether a subscription is active.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's data is included in that backup like any other app, under Apple's privacy policy. We have no copy. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete — it is all on your device and under your control. Questions are still welcome: developers9x@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change. If the app ever starts sending data off your device, this page will say so first and the app will ask you.

This page describes the app as built. It is not legal advice.
